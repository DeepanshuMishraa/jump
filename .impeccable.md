## Design Context

### Users
Keyboard-first Chromium users who keep many tabs open and need to switch quickly without leaving their current task.

### Brand Personality
Fast, focused, and native. Jump should feel dependable and quiet rather than flashy.

### Aesthetic Direction
A flagship-quality native Mac utility: compact dimensions, tinted dark and light surfaces, crisp typography, restrained color, clear keyboard cues, and minimal motion. Avoid terminal cosplay, decorative gradients, oversized controls, and generic card-heavy layouts.

### Design Principles
- Keep the primary choice obvious and immediately interactive.
- Use spacing, type, and subtle dividers instead of nested cards.
- Show shortcuts where they help without competing with the main control.
- Preserve strong contrast, visible focus states, and reduced-motion support.
- Prefer optimistic feedback and stable dimensions so the popup feels instant.
